About the project

A place to live,
a place to work.

A small hillside house in Sikkim for a fashion designer who lives alone. The brief asked for a home to live in and a studio to work in comfortably, under one roof.

  1. 01

    Living above, working below

    The fashion studio sits at the base; the kitchen, personal room and bedroom rise above it, away from the work.

  2. 02

    One stair, every level

    A single continuous stair ties the studio to the home, so work is a few steps away and still apart.

  3. 03

    Built into the slope

    The house steps with the hillside, with rooms below ground and a garden at the entrance.
